How to Use This Calculator

Select your state, experience band, primary certification, and market segment. Stack additional certifications to see combined premium estimates. The calculator returns P25, median, and P75 annual salary ranges based on 2026 BLS OEWS and H-1B LCA benchmarks.

Why Certification Matters

Salary benchmarks for solar project managers vary most by three factors: state cost of labor, years of experience, and certifications held. PMP and NABCEP PVIP show measurable premium impact; OSHA 30 functions as a compliance gate rather than an independent premium. When stacked, OSHA 30 applies a small structural uplift (+2%) and an electrical license +4%; combined certification premiums are capped at +30%.

Formula and Data Sources

Range equals BLS OEWS state benchmark (occupation 11-9021) times experience coefficient times certification premium matrix. Premiums: PMP +$20K to $30K (SurgePV); NABCEP PVIP +$3 to $8 per hour (skillit, 2080-hour annual reference); OSHA 30 Required or Preferred (IECA, Mortenson, PEA). Version 2026.11.

Applicability Boundaries

OSHA 30 has no independent salary premium evidence; it is displayed as Required or Preferred only, and stacking it applies a small structural uplift (+2%) to the calculated band. NABCEP hourly premiums are converted to annual display at 2,080 hours for reference only. All figures are estimates and do not constitute an employment offer or guarantee.

Data Provenance

BLS Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS), H-1B LCA state-level aggregates, SurgePV PMP premium survey, skillit NABCEP hourly data, IECA, Mortenson, and Philadelphia Energy Authority OSHA 30 job postings.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the solar project manager salary calculated?

Salary ranges combine BLS OEWS occupation 11-9021 state benchmarks, H-1B LCA state means, and a certification premium matrix covering PMP, NABCEP PVIP, OSHA 30, and electrical licenses. Estimates only, not an employment offer.

Does OSHA 30 add a salary premium?

OSHA 30 is a safety compliance requirement, listed as Required or Preferred by segment. There is no independent OSHA 30 salary premium evidence; when stacked with other credentials it applies a small structural uplift (+2%) to the calculated band, and an electrical license applies +4%. Combined certification premiums are capped at +30%.

What is the average solar project manager salary?

The national median solar project manager salary is approximately $105,000 per year, with P25 around $88,000 and P75 around $128,000. Actual ranges vary by state, experience, and certification.
