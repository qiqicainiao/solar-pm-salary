What the data is and is not

Salary figures are estimates derived from voluntarily submitted corrections, public postings and modelled adjustments. They are aggregated ranges, not offers of employment, and they are not career, legal or financial advice. Sample sizes vary by state and certification; the tool shows the sample size behind every range so you can judge how much weight to give it.
