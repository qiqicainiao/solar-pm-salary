Refund Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

The short version

14 days, no questions asked. If you are not satisfied with a Pro subscription, ask within 14 days of the charge and you get your money back in full — whether or not you have used the tool.

How to request one

Either route works, and neither requires a reason:

  • Email 269715607@qq.com from the address you paid with, and include the order or invoice number from your receipt. We reply within two business days.
  • Open the receipt email sent by Creem and use the refund link in it, or contact Creem support directly and quote your order number.

Creem is the merchant of record for all purchases, so the refund itself is issued by Creem back to your original payment method. You do not need to open a dispute with your bank or card issuer. In fact, please contact us first — a chargeback takes longer for you and costs both sides a fee.

How long it takes

Refunds are approved within two business days of your request. Once issued, the money typically appears on your statement within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer — that timing is outside our control and outside Creem's.

Cancelling is not the same as refunding

This trips people up, so it is worth stating plainly:

  • Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for. No money moves.
  • Refunding returns the money for a charge already taken, and Pro access ends when the refund is issued.

You can cancel at any time from the link in your receipt email. If you cancel and later decide you want the last charge back too, ask within the 14-day window and we will refund it.

Renewals

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. We do not send a reminder before each renewal, so if you only needed the tool for one project, cancel as soon as you are done — you keep access to the end of the paid period either way. A renewal charge within the last 14 days is refundable on the same terms as a first charge.

Your statutory rights

Nothing here reduces rights you have under the consumer law of your own country. Consumers in the European Union and the United Kingdom have a 14-day right of withdrawal for distance purchases; where that right applies, this policy grants it to you in full and does not require you to have avoided using the service. If local law gives you a longer period, the longer period applies.
