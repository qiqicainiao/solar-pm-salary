Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What We Collect

With your consent, we collect anonymous, non-personal usage data to improve salary estimate accuracy:

  • Your selections in the salary calculator (state, experience band, certification, segment) — these are fixed enum values, not free text.
  • Whether you confirmed, corrected, or reported a missing estimate — corrections are collected as salary buckets (e.g. $75–100K), never exact dollar amounts.
  • Interaction metadata (page views, time spent, whether you expanded the certification detail).

How We Use It

Collected data is used solely to improve salary estimate algorithms and data quality. It is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

Retention

Collected event data is retained for approximately 180 days, after which it is aggregated or deleted.

Consent and Your Choices

Consent works in three states: before you choose, every page load sends one minimal anonymous visit event — no cookie, no session identifier, no browser storage and no cross-session link — used only to count page visits in aggregate; if you accept, we also record the detailed events described above under a site-scoped session reference; if you decline, no analytics requests are sent at all.

You may accept or reject using the consent banner on the calculator. To withdraw consent at any time, use the “Cookie settings” entry in the footer (it clears vr_consent_v1 and the site session reference) or clear this site's browser storage.
